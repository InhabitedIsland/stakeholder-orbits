Stakeholder Orbits

Score the 8 to 12 people who can actually sink your project, once a month, in about five minutes. Watch where they move. The tool compares their trajectories with patterns that usually precede success or trouble.

The log

Scores

Influence and interest from 1 to 5, stance from opposed to champion. A new period copies the last one, so you only change who moved. Write down why in your head, or in the name field if you must.

Twelve is the limit. If everyone matters, nobody does. Merge people into groups or retire someone who no longer moves the needle.

The atlas

Patterns to recognise

Sixteen trajectories drawn from project practice. They are field notes, not statistics: use them to ask better questions, not to pass verdicts. Patterns visible in your project right now are outlined.

Back up or move your data

Scores of colleagues' stance are sensitive. This file runs entirely on your computer and sends nothing anywhere: your scores are kept only in this browser. Clearing your browser data erases them, so copy a backup now and then and keep it somewhere safe.

How to read the map

Position matters less than movement. A sponsor in the top right is fine; a sponsor sliding left for three months is not. The colour changes are often the earliest signal, because people change their minds before they change their behaviour.

Remember the observer problem. You score what you perceive. When someone appears to move, either they changed or you finally noticed. Both are worth knowing.

Each finding shows when it first became visible. Scrub back in time and ask: would I have acted in that month?
